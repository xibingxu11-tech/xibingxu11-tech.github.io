Research
focus

My work follows a common question across different systems: how do bacteria reprogram translation and protein organization when growth becomes costly or dangerous?

01 · RNA

tRNA-targeting toxins

Discovered how MenT-family nucleotidyltransferases extend tRNA acceptor ends, deplete chargeable serine tRNAs and reshape translation in Mycobacterium tuberculosis.

02 · REGULATION

Phosphorylation as a toxin switch

Revealed inducible toxin auto-phosphorylation and investigated the evolutionary diversification of AbiE-related toxin–antitoxin systems.

03 · RIBOSOME

Precision attacks on translation

Contributed to mechanistic studies of ribosome-directed toxins, including RNases that cleave the anti–Shine–Dalgarno region of 16S rRNA.

04 · AMR

Membrane organization in MRSA

Current work at CNB-CSIC examines FloA-dependent functional membrane microdomains, resistance-protein organization and membrane proteostasis.
